What we do

The filter, not the deal.

When an owner sells a private business, their adviser approaches a shortlist of buyers directly. Details are shared only after a non-disclosure agreement is signed.

We bring family offices and private buyers off-market businesses for sale, and property, in the UK and overseas. Every opportunity is checked before it reaches you, so your time goes only on deals that are real and that fit.

We work with buyers seeking the whole company or a controlling stake (50% or more). Minority stakes are referred to authorised partners.

What we check

What we check before an introduction.

THE NUMBERS

Revenue, margins, cash, working capital and forecasts.

THE STORY

Whether the owner’s account matches the evidence.

WHAT A BUYER NEEDS TO KNOW

Customer concentration, owner dependency, legal matters, intellectual property and key people.

THE MATCH

Whether the business fits what you want to acquire.

What you receive

An information memorandum, before any introduction.

It covers the history and ownership of the business; customers, suppliers and contracts; the last two to three years of accounts; forecasts and cash flow; key people; legal matters and property.

You then carry out your own due diligence before any offer is final.
