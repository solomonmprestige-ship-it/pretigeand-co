What we do

Ready for a buyer’s checks.

If you are selling your business, preparing it for sale takes longer than you might expect. A buyer checks your accounts, contracts, staff and legal position before agreeing a price. If your business is ready for those checks, you are more likely to achieve your price.

We get your business ready, then advise you through to completion, whether you sell the whole company or a controlling interest.

Investors run the same checks before putting in development capital, so the same preparation applies if you are raising money to grow. We prepare your business; the raise itself is handled by authorised partners.

How a sale works

How a sale works.

1

PREPARATION

We prepare your business and check it the way a buyer will.

2

INFORMATION MEMORANDUM

The document buyers receive: your history, customers, accounts, forecasts and key people.

3

SHORTLIST OF BUYERS

We draw up a list of suitable buyers and approach them directly. Every buyer is vetted first.

4

NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Buyers sign one before they see any detail.

5

OFFERS

Interested buyers make offers, and the strongest go forward.

6

HEADS OF TERMS

The agreed outline of the deal: price, structure and timing.

7

DUE DILIGENCE

The buyer checks your business commercially, financially and legally.

8

SALE AGREEMENT

Your solicitors and the buyer’s agree the final contract.

9

COMPLETION

The sale goes through.

How we work with you

Discretion. Integrity. Diligence.

DISCRETION

Your plans stay private. Buyers see detailed information only after signing a non-disclosure agreement, so your competitors, customers and staff hear of it only when you choose.

INTEGRITY

You hear what a buyer would find, including where your value can be improved. If you need a lawyer, tax adviser or regulated firm, we say so and refer you.

DILIGENCE

We check your business commercially, financially and legally, as a buyer would.
