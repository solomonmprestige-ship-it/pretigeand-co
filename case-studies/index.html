Case study 01

Investor & Builder

All Clear Solutions

Marina Alta, Spain · Waste management & property services

From man-and-van to waste management company.

THE STARTING POINT

I took over a man-and-van business. It ran on free house clearances, paid for by reselling the furniture it collected. The model was flawed, so we rebuilt it.

THE REBUILD

Repositioned as a paid, documented waste management company, aligned with Spain’s 2022 Waste Law: every load documented to an authorised facility. Five defined services. A new pricing structure. New brand, new website, automated booking.

THE ROUTE TO MARKET

A referral network of estate agents, solicitors and property managers. Localised marketing and lead generation across nine Marina Alta towns.

THE STANDARD

Through the change, one focus: service excellence. Every job done properly, every client asked to say so publicly.

THE RESULT

A team of four now delivers the service. 100% recommend across 25 reviews on Facebook (October 2026), built while the business moved from furniture resale to waste management.

NEXT
