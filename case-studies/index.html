Client All Clear Solutions
Sector Environmental Services
Service Investability Assessment & Capital Advisory

Preparing a specialist environmental services business for its first institutional raise.

The situation

All Clear Solutions had built a strong operational business in environmental remediation and site clearance — recurring revenues, a solid client base, and a clear expansion opportunity in a market with growing regulatory tailwinds. The founders had identified a capital partner and were preparing to open a formal conversation. They engaged Prestige & Co to review the business before that process began.

What we found

The investability assessment identified three material areas that would have created problems in investor due diligence: a revenue concentration issue that understated the true spread of the client base; a financial model with projection assumptions that would not hold under the standard stress-testing an institutional investor would apply; and a documentation gap in the legal structure that needed to be resolved before a formal process could proceed cleanly.

None of these were fatal to the raise — but each would have been surfaced during due diligence, and each would have given a prospective investor grounds to renegotiate terms or introduce conditions. Finding them first meant they were addressed on the business's own timetable, not under pressure from a live process.

What we did

We worked with the founders to restate the revenue analysis in a form that accurately reflected customer diversity while remaining consistent with the underlying data. We rebuilt the financial model's projection methodology to a standard that would withstand institutional scrutiny. We identified the legal issue to the business's solicitors and ensured it was resolved before the investor was approached.

We then supported the founders through the early investor conversations, providing commercial direction as the process developed.

The outcome

All Clear Solutions entered its institutional fundraise in a materially stronger position than it would have been without the preparation. The due diligence process ran without the disruptions that the pre-assessment findings would have caused. The raise completed on terms consistent with the founders' original expectations.
