Sale Readiness Assessment — an exit readiness assessment

What a buyer’s checks would find, before a buyer sees your business.

Discretion. Integrity. Diligence.

DiscretionPrivate information remains private. Nothing shared without your permission. Opportunities held for a private, trusted few.

IntegrityHonest advice. Straight answers. Nothing hidden.

DiligenceEvery detail checked. Every party verified. Both sides protected.

Find it before the buyer does.

An exit readiness assessment for your business before you sell. It finds what a buyer’s checks would find, commercially, financially and legally. Starting early gives you time to act on it.

WHAT WE LOOK AT

History and ownership · Customers, suppliers and contracts · Trading performance, accounts and forecasts · Property and assets · Key people · Legal matters.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A list of actions in order of priority: your checklist for preparing a business for sale. We prepare your business for vendor due diligence (the formal report a seller commissions for buyers); we do not produce that report ourselves.

WHEN TO USE IT

As early as possible before a sale, so there is time to act on what it finds.

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

Discretion: your sale stays private until you decide otherwise.

Integrity: every finding is set out plainly.

Diligence: we check your business as thoroughly as a buyer would.
