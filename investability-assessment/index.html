The fact find, for your business.

An investment readiness assessment for your business. It is a fact find: it shows where your business stands, commercially and financially, and what needs doing before a buyer or investor sees it.

WHAT WE LOOK AT

Your business model and market · Trading history and forecasts · Cash flow and what the money is for · Ownership and structure · Key people · Contracts and legal position.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A clear picture of what a buyer or investor will focus on, and a list of what to address first. If a deeper check is needed, due diligence is a separate, paid service.

WHEN TO USE IT

As the first step before a sale, or before you approach investors. Raising capital for a small business? Preparation starts here. The raise itself is handled by authorised partners.

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

Discretion: what you share stays with us.

Integrity: every point is stated plainly.

Diligence: we check your business as a buyer or investor would.
