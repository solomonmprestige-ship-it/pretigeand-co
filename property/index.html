Property — Corporate Finance for Developers, Landlords & Investors.

Discretion. Integrity. Diligence.

We check it properly before anyone puts money behind it — and tell you the truth about it either way.

Developing a site, holding a portfolio, or looking to invest in real estate: the work is the same. We review the numbers, the assumptions, and the project before anyone else sees it. If it stacks up, we present it in the form that investors want to receive it. If it does not, we tell you what needs to change.

We have a global real estate network built through active deal origination and placement since 2005. Over that period we have placed real estate opportunities with family offices, institutional desks, and private investors across the UK and internationally. The network is built on relationships, not directories — and every introduction through it is preceded by a proper review.

Property Sourcing is a material part of what we do — not just a listed service name. We identify development sites, portfolio assets, and off-market opportunities for investors with specific acquisition briefs. The sourcing is done through a professional intermediary network built over 20 years: estate agents, planning consultants, solicitors, insolvency practitioners, and lenders who bring us opportunities before they are marketed.

Who we work with

Property Developers

You have a site, a planning consent, or a scheme underway and need investors who understand real estate. We review the numbers, prepare the opportunity properly, and introduce it to the right people.

Landlords & Portfolio Holders

You hold real estate and want an honest assessment of value, or guidance on how to position it for sale or refinance. We tell you what it is worth and what would need to change to move that number.

Real Estate Investors

You are looking to invest and want properly vetted opportunities. We introduce opportunities to a defined group of investors — when we make an introduction, the work has already been done.

Three referred services

Valuation, finance arranging, and legal work are regulated activities. We refer them to qualified specialists from our professional network — and remain the point of commercial continuity throughout, so the client has one coherent team across all workstreams.

Referred · Regulated activity

Valuation

Where a formal RICS valuation is required — for finance, sale, or investment purposes — we introduce clients to qualified valuers from our professional network. We brief the appointed firm and stay across the process commercially.

Referred · Regulated activity

Finance Arranging

Where mortgage, development finance, or bridging is required, we introduce clients to specialist brokers and lenders. Arranging regulated credit requires FCA authorisation — our role is commercial preparation and introduction; the broker handles the regulated advice.

Referred · Regulated activity

Legal & Contracts

Where transaction legal work is required, we introduce clients to specialist property solicitors. Legal advice requires SRA authorisation — our role is commercial coordination; the solicitor handles the legal work. We ensure the commercial and legal workstreams are aligned.
