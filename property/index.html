Global Property Consultants

Discretion. Integrity. Diligence.

Global property consultants for developers, landlords and investors, in the UK and fifteen markets. Lenders, buyers and partners check the numbers, the planning position and the people behind a deal before they commit. We get your project ready.

What we do for you

  • Property Sourcing: development sites, investment property and off-market opportunities, in the UK and abroad.
  • Capital Verification & Deal Assessments: checking the money behind a deal is real and the numbers work.
  • Lender Intelligence & Market Insights: which lenders are active, and on what terms.
  • Portfolio Exit Planning: planning the sale of a property or portfolio.
  • Deal Support: transaction coordination through to completion. Legal work by a partner solicitor.
  • Market Intelligence Reports: pricing, yields, planning and lender appetite in your market.

Need finance for property development, bridging finance, private finance for property or finance for property investment? It’s arranged by an authorised broker; we make the introduction. Valuations are carried out by a RICS-qualified surveyor, and legal work by a solicitor in the relevant country.

Where we work

The United Kingdom, Spain, Portugal, France, Italy, Germany, Poland, the Czech Republic, Albania, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, the United States, Mexico and Dubai (UAE). In each market, we work with local agents, developers, lawyers and brokers.

How we work with you

  • Discretion. Your plans and figures stay private.
  • Integrity. Every point is stated plainly.
  • Diligence. Every opportunity is checked before it reaches you.
