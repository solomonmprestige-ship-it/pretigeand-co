Three referred services

Valuation, finance arranging, and legal work are regulated activities. We refer them to qualified specialists from our professional network — and remain the point of commercial continuity throughout, so the client has one coherent team across all workstreams.

Referred · Regulated activity

Valuation

Where a formal RICS valuation is required — for finance, sale, or investment purposes — we introduce clients to qualified valuers from our professional network. We brief the appointed firm and stay across the process commercially.

Referred · Regulated activity

Finance Arranging

Where mortgage, development finance, or bridging is required, we introduce clients to specialist brokers and lenders. Arranging regulated credit requires FCA authorisation — our role is commercial preparation and introduction; the broker handles the regulated advice.

Referred · Regulated activity

Legal & Contracts

Where transaction legal work is required, we introduce clients to specialist property solicitors. Legal advice requires SRA authorisation — our role is commercial coordination; the solicitor handles the legal work. We ensure the commercial and legal workstreams are aligned.
