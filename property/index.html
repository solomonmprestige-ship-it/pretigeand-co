Finance and specialists

Regulated work goes to regulated firms.

Need finance for property development, bridging finance, private finance for property or finance for property investment? It is arranged by an authorised broker; we make the introduction.

Valuations are carried out by a RICS-qualified surveyor, and legal work by a solicitor in the relevant country.
