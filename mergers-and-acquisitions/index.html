Mergers & Acquisitions — boutique M&A advisory, UK

The whole company or a controlling stake, through to completion.

Discretion. Integrity. Diligence.

DiscretionPrivate information remains private. Nothing shared without your permission. Opportunities held for a private, trusted few.

IntegrityHonest advice. Straight answers. Nothing hidden.

DiligenceEvery detail checked. Every party verified. Both sides protected.

Selling or buying. Vetted on both sides.

Boutique M&A advisory in the UK, whether you are selling or buying a whole business or a controlling interest (50% or more). As a small business M&A advisor, we work on both the sell side and the buy side.

IF YOU ARE SELLING

We prepare your business and the information memorandum (the document buyers receive), draw up a list of suitable buyers, vet them, and run the process under non-disclosure agreements. We then advise you through negotiation and heads of terms (the agreed outline of the deal) to completion, alongside your solicitors.

IF YOU ARE BUYING

We vet the business, support your checks, and advise you through to completion.

WHAT WE REFER

Minority stakes and development capital are referred to authorised partners.

WHEN TO USE IT

When you have decided to sell or buy, or when an offer arrives and you want advice before you respond.

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

Discretion: buyers receive the information memorandum only after signing a non-disclosure agreement.

Integrity: your information memorandum describes your business accurately.

Diligence: we check the business commercially, financially and legally before terms are agreed.
