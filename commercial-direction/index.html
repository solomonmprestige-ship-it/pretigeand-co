Commercial Direction

Senior counsel in the room for the calls that matter.

There are moments in a business's life where the quality of the commercial thinking in the room makes a material difference to the outcome. A live fundraise. A term sheet negotiation. A conversation with a potential acquirer. A partnership agreement that sets the terms of a relationship for years. These are not moments for process; they are moments for judgement.

Commercial Direction provides exactly that — direct, senior commercial involvement at the moments when it counts. We work alongside founders and business leaders during live transactions and critical commercial decisions, providing experienced counsel and active participation in the conversations themselves.

This is not advisory at arm's length. We are in the room — on the calls, in the meetings, reading the term sheets, and engaged in the negotiations. Our involvement is direct and ongoing for the duration of the engagement.

When we are typically engaged

  • During active fundraising processes — from first investor conversations through to term sheet and close
  • In sale processes — advising on negotiation strategy and participating in key discussions with buyers
  • On term sheet review — providing an independent, experienced read of what has been offered and what should be pushed back on
  • In JV and partnership negotiations — ensuring the commercial terms reflect the actual balance of leverage
  • Ahead of and during board presentations where capital or strategic decisions are on the agenda

Commercial Direction engagements are structured to the specific situation — the scope, the timeline, and the nature of the involvement are agreed at the outset. We work with a small number of clients at any time to ensure the attention given is direct and undivided.

Everything is held in strict confidence throughout.
