Who we are

Independent. Boutique. Built on the fact find.

Independent corporate finance advisory for owners of established UK businesses, for the buyers and investors who want to acquire them, and for property developers, landlords and investors.

We are a corporate finance boutique that has sat where you are sitting. We have founded a business, raised investment and sold it. We have run fact finds as financial planners. We have spent five years sourcing and checking property deals across the UK and Europe.

That is why we work the way we do: the facts first, then the deal.

Our approach

The same process for everyone.

Every business goes through the same process before it reaches a buyer: an application, a screening stage, and an Investability Assessment.

Buyers go through the same process: proof of funds, and a fact find on what they want to acquire. Property deals are checked the same way before they reach you.

Buyers only see opportunities that have been checked. Businesses are only introduced to buyers who fit.
