Prestige & Co is a boutique, independent corporate finance firm.

Discretion. Integrity. Diligence.

Independent corporate finance advisory for owners of established UK businesses, for the buyers and investors who want to acquire them, and for property developers, landlords and investors. We bring 25 years of experience across retail banking, wealth management, mergers and acquisitions, business-building and real estate.

Trained by the Chartered Insurance Institute in discretionary investment management, financial planning and mortgage advice, and by the Chartered Institute for Securities & Investment in corporate finance.

What we bring

We’re a corporate finance boutique that has sat where you’re sitting.

  • We’ve founded a business, raised investment and sold it.
  • We’ve completed more than ten acquisitions and exits of financial advice firms.
  • We’ve built sales from nothing: 100 calls a day, pitching investors, hearing no.
  • We’ve run fact finds as financial planners: the hard facts (income, assets, liabilities) and the soft facts, like attitude to risk, that decide whether a deal is right for you.
  • We’ve worked at institutional level, as a broker consultant in wealth management.
  • We’ve spent five years in real estate, sourcing and checking property deals across the UK and Europe.

Our approach

Every business goes through the same process before it reaches a buyer or investor: an application, a screening stage, and an Investability Assessment. Buyers go through the same process: proof of funds, and a fact find on what they want to acquire. Property deals are checked the same way before they reach you. Buyers only see opportunities that have been checked, and businesses are only introduced to buyers who fit.

How we work with you

  • Discretion. Your identity and your plans stay private.
  • Integrity. Every point is stated plainly, and we act in your interest.
  • Diligence. We check the facts before anything reaches a buyer or investor.
