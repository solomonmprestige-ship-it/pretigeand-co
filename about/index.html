We are a boutique UK corporate finance consultancy. We have spent 25 years on every side of the table — founding businesses, raising capital, advising on transactions, and executing deals. We bring that experience directly to bear for the people we work with.

Discretion. Integrity. Diligence.

Discretion

Nothing about a client's business goes further than it needs to. We operate on a strict need-to-know basis. Confidential information stays confidential — before, during, and after an engagement.

Integrity

We tell clients the truth, including when it is not what they want to hear. If the numbers do not stack, we say so. If the business is not ready, we say so. We would rather lose a mandate than give bad advice.

Diligence

We check everything properly before it leaves our hands. Every number. Every assumption. Every claim. Because the professionals on the other side of the table will, and we ensure there are no surprises.

Drawing on 25 years across every side of the transaction.

Most advisers have worked on one side of the table. They have advised founders, or they have worked for an investment firm, or they have practised in a professional services firm. That is the lens through which they see every transaction.

We have worked on all sides. We have been the founder raising capital and the adviser managing the process. We have been the party preparing for sale and the one conducting due diligence. We have placed capital on behalf of institutional investors and presented opportunities to family offices. That breadth changes how we work.

When we review a business ahead of a fundraise, we are drawing on direct experience of what the investor's team will do when they receive the deck. We know which numbers will be stress-tested, which assumptions will be questioned, and which gaps in the financial model will stall a deal. We find those things before the investor does.

When we prepare a business for sale, we think like the buyer's advisers. We have sat across the table from a vendor's team more than once. We know what the due diligence process surfaces, how a buyer uses those findings to renegotiate price, and what a clean, well-prepared deal looks like by comparison.

When we introduce an opportunity to a capital partner, we know what that partner expects to receive and in what form. We have placed real estate with family offices and institutional desks. We have introduced growth businesses to investors who have backed them. Those introductions are made after thorough preparation — not before.

The common thread is preparation. Every engagement begins with an honest assessment of where the client stands and what the other party will conclude when they look at it properly. We do not dress up a business for presentation. We identify what needs to be addressed and address it, so the business speaks for itself.

We operate from London. The clients we work with are based across the UK and internationally. The capital partners and investors we work with are UK-focused, with selective international reach through established relationships.

The practice is deliberately small. We take on a limited number of engagements at any one time so that every client receives direct senior attention throughout. There are no junior-led workstreams. There is no handover to a team once the mandate is agreed. The same people who agree the mandate deliver the work.

Fees are structured to reflect the work done, not the outcome of a transaction. We do not structure our compensation in a way that creates an incentive to push a transaction through regardless of whether it is the right outcome. We are paid for our work. If the right outcome is to delay, restructure, or not proceed, we say so.

Confidentiality is absolute. Nothing about a client's business is shared beyond the people and parties who need to know it, and then only with the client's explicit knowledge and agreement. We do not discuss client matters. We do not use clients as references without their permission. The work stays between us.

We work across three markets: business leaders who are raising capital or preparing for a transaction; investors who want properly vetted deal flow presented in a form they can act on; and the property sector, where we provide the same rigour to development finance and real estate investment that we apply to business transactions.

The services we offer reflect the work that actually moves transactions forward. Investability assessments. Sale readiness reviews. Financial reality sessions. Commercial direction. Operating partner engagements. M&A mandates. Each has been developed from direct experience of what the other side of the table looks for and what makes the difference between a transaction that completes and one that does not.

The twenty-five years are not a marketing figure. They represent specific experience across a specific range of transaction types, capital markets, and business situations. We draw on all of it in every engagement.

We are straightforward to work with. We say what we mean. We do what we say we will do. We tell clients when we think they are wrong. We charge fairly for the work we do. We keep everything confidential.

If that is the kind of adviser you are looking for, we would be glad to speak with you.

Our approach to business.

We take on a small number of clients at any given time. Not because of capacity constraints, but because we believe that good advisory work requires focus. Every client we work with receives our direct attention, not a delegated version of it.

We begin every engagement with an honest conversation about where the client stands. That sometimes means telling people things they would rather not hear. We think that is more useful than telling them what they want to hear and billing for the privilege.

We do not work with clients we cannot help. If the business is not at a stage where our work will make a material difference, we say so. If a transaction is not viable in its current form, we say so. We would rather pass on a mandate than take it and deliver a poor outcome.

We measure our success by whether our clients close the transactions they set out to close, on terms that reflect the real value of what they are offering. Everything we do is aimed at that outcome.
