Our approach to corporate finance advisory.

Every business we work with goes through the same process before it ever reaches investors: an application form, a screening stage, and an Investability Assessment. On the investor side, the same discipline applies — proof of funds, and fact-finding to understand exactly what they're looking for before any introduction is made. This means investors only see opportunities that have already been checked, and businesses are only introduced to investors who actually fit what they're looking for — saving time on both sides.
