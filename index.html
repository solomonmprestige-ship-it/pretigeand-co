How we work

A considered approach to buying and selling businesses.

Most opportunities are shared widely before anyone checks them. We work the other way. Off market, held for a private, trusted few, and vetted before anything leaves the room.

THE FACT FIND

Every engagement starts with a fact find, as financial planners do. Your goals, your timescale, and where this sale or purchase sits in your wider plans. Only then do we look at the deal.

SELLERS VETTED

Accounts, ownership, contracts and key people, checked the way a buyer will check them. Where the owner’s story and the evidence differ, we find it first.

BUYERS VETTED

Proof of funds, and a clear picture of what they want to acquire. Businesses are only introduced to buyers who fit.

BROKERS VETTED

Who they are, who they act for, and how many people have already seen the deal.

Who we work with

Business leaders. Investors. Property.

We work with owner-managers, acquirers and property developers across the UK and overseas. One trusted contact, from the first conversation to completion.

BUSINESS LEADERS

If you are planning to sell your company, or a controlling interest, we prepare the business for a buyer’s checks and advise you through to completion. Raising development capital? We prepare the business; the raise itself is handled by authorised partners.

INVESTORS

If you are a family office or private buyer seeking a controlling interest in an established business, tell us what you are looking for. We will be in touch when a checked opportunity fits.

PROPERTY

If you are developing, growing a portfolio, selling, or buying abroad, we check every opportunity before it reaches you. Finance comes through authorised brokers, and valuations through RICS surveyors.

The founder

Solomon Prestige.

Twenty-five years across banking, wealth management, mergers and acquisitions, business-building and real estate. Trained by the Chartered Insurance Institute, and studying corporate finance with the Chartered Institute for Securities & Investment.

That background is what Prestige & Co is built on: understanding how businesses are valued, how transactions are structured, and how buyers and sellers reach agreement.

This is a commercial consultancy. We do not give regulated investment advice, arrange capital raises or hold client money. What we do is prepare businesses, vet every party, and put the right buyer in front of the right opportunity.
