Frequently Asked

What Founders, Owner-Managers and Property Developers Ask Most

How do I prepare for a capital raise in the UK?

The work that gets you the yes is done before the meeting, not in it. Numbers that hold up under due diligence. A pitch deck that puts what matters first. A founder who walks into the room with the answer ready to every question they will be asked. That is what Capital Raise Preparation does — so the odds of a yes are as high as they can be when you walk in.

What is exit readiness?

The work done before sale, so the business shows its true worth to a buyer. To clean the numbers a buyer will look at. To strengthen anything that depends too heavily on one person, one customer, or one contract. To walk into the sale with nothing for the buyer to push back on — and the offer comes in at the top end of what it should be, not the bottom.

How do I prepare a UK business for sale?

Start earlier than you think. There is real work to do before any buyer is in the room — fixing the things a buyer's team will use to push the valuation down, before they ever come up. The Exit Readiness Review is the structured way to do that work, so the price you finally accept is the one your years are worth, not the one you settled for.

What do investors look for in due diligence?

Clean numbers. A credible model. A coherent story. A founder who can answer hard questions without flinching. Due diligence readiness is built into Capital Raise Preparation from day one — not bolted on at the end.

What does a fractional CFO do?

A fractional CFO brings senior financial leadership into a business without the cost of a full-time hire. Strategy, structure, the calls that move the numbers. At Prestige & Co, the equivalent service is Commercial Direction — counsel beside you when the big decisions get made, twenty-five years of experience in the room, without a senior salary on the books.

Is Prestige & Co regulated by the FCA?

No. Prestige & Co is a commercial advisory consultancy, not a regulated firm. We do not arrange investments. We do not provide regulated financial advice. Where regulated advice is required, we refer to appropriately authorised partners. The work we do is the commercial preparation that gets the deal to the table — not the transaction itself.
