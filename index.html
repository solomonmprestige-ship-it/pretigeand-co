Approach

UK Corporate Advisory — Commercial Preparation, Not Regulated Advice

Most businesses walk into a capital raise or a sale without having had their numbers read back to them plainly. The result is predictable — diligence bites, offers land on the wrong terms, and the seat at the table is the wrong one.

The work here starts earlier. Preparing financials that hold up under scrutiny. Identifying the dependencies a founder has stopped seeing. Framing what a business is actually worth — to whom, and why. The groundwork that makes a later conversation a negotiation rather than a reaction.

This is commercial consultancy. Not regulated advice. That distinction is clear in everything we do.

Common questions

What founders and developers ask before getting in touch.

What does a UK corporate finance consultant actually do?

Prepares businesses for capital raises, exits, and major commercial decisions. Financial modelling, investor narrative, exit readiness, and commercial direction. Commercial consultancy — not regulated financial advice.

How much does a Financial Reality Session cost?

£2,500 fixed. A single session. A plain commercial read of where your business actually stands — honest findings, no retainer attached.

Is Prestige & Co regulated by the FCA?

No. We are a commercial consultancy. Not FCA authorised. We do not provide regulated financial advice or hold client money. Where regulated advice is required, we refer to appropriately authorised partners.

What stage does a business need to be at?

Well-funded and past proof-of-concept. Series A readiness, or equivalent. For property mandates, significant development or portfolio — not single-unit transactions.

How does an engagement start?

A direct conversation via WhatsApp or email. If there is a fit, the engagement is scoped and priced clearly before anything begins. If there is not, that is said plainly.

Do you work with pre-revenue businesses?

No. The work here is most valuable when a business already has revenue, users, or committed capital — and needs to prepare properly for what comes next.
