What your business is worth today.

How much is my business worth? The Financial Reality Session answers that: an informed view of what your business is worth today, based on the figures a buyer would use. It is not a formal business valuation.

WHAT WE LOOK AT

Your profits and how reliable they are · Cash flow · Debt · How much depends on a few customers · How much depends on you.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Where your value sits, and what would raise it before a sale. If you need a formal valuation, for tax, a dispute or a shareholder matter, we refer you to a qualified valuer.

WHEN TO USE IT

Before you decide whether to sell, when an offer arrives, or when you want to know what would raise the value.

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

Discretion: your figures stay confidential.

Integrity: your value is based on the evidence a buyer would use.

Diligence: every figure is checked, not taken at face value.
