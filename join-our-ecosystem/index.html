Two ways to join

Introducers. Panel members.

We work with accountants, solicitors, independent financial advisers, wealth managers, property agents, developers, landowners, valuers and brokers. Every introduction is checked the same way we check everything.

BECOME AN INTRODUCER

You know a business owner preparing to sell, a buyer looking to acquire, a site worth developing, or a portfolio coming to market. You make the introduction. We take it from there. Where it leads to a paid engagement, you share in our advisory fee, agreed in writing under a signed Sub-Introducer Agreement before anything moves. You are not expected to negotiate, handle money, or advise on anything.

JOIN THE PANEL

We refer regulated work out as a matter of course: valuation to RICS surveyors, finance to FCA-authorised brokers, legal work to solicitors in the relevant country. If you cover a market we work in, we want you on the list. The work is real and it is regular.
