Professionals In Partnership

Join our ecosystem

Introducers, partners and specialists across fifteen markets.

Prestige & Co works through relationships, not advertising. Most of what reaches us comes from people who already know the client, the site, or the market.

If that’s you, we would like to hear from you.

Who we work with

Professional intermediaries

Accountants, solicitors, independent financial advisers and wealth managers with clients selling a business, buying property abroad, or preparing for investment. Your client stays your client. We report back to you, and where your client needs regulated advice, it comes straight back to you or to the right firm in your market.

Property and land introducers

Agents, developers and landowners with a site, a standing asset or a portfolio. We hold signed mandates with investors, funds and family offices looking at real estate. If what you have fits one of them, we can tell you quickly.

Specialist partners

RICS valuers, FCA-authorised brokers and property lawyers in the markets we work in. Where a client needs regulated work, we introduce it. We are looking for firms who can take that on in each jurisdiction.

How it works

  1. 01 You tell us what you have, or who you know.
  2. 02 We check it. The same screening as everything else that reaches us.
  3. 03 If we take it on, we agree the terms with you in writing first.
  4. 04 We keep you informed throughout.

What you get

We work on a simple principle: where you are the catalyst for revenue we generate, we share it. You receive an agreed share of our net commission, paid within 14 days of the funds clearing on a completed transaction.

Every introduction is confirmed by us in writing within five working days, so there is never a question later about who brought what. The arrangement runs for an agreed tail period, so a deal that takes a year to complete still earns you the fee.

It runs both ways. Where our client needs a lawyer, an accountant, a valuer or a broker in your market, that work goes to you. We refer regulated work out as a matter of course, so those introductions are real and regular.

Terms are set out in a signed Sub-Introducer Agreement before any introduction is made.

Two ways to join the ecosystem.

Become an introducer.

You know a business looking for capital, a site, a portfolio, or a client selling up. You make the introduction, we take it from there, and you share in what it earns. Terms in writing, under a signed Sub-Introducer Agreement.
